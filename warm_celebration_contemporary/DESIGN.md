## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the warmth, richness, and celebratory vibrancy of premium Kenyan hospitality rooted in Eldoret and the Great Rift Valley. It balances high-end bespoke event production with welcoming, communal warmth—moving away from generic corporate sterility into an editorial, tactile, and festive digital atmosphere.

### Emotional Resonance & Aesthetic Style
- **Visual Theme**: An organic synthesis of warm editorial luxury and modern Kenyan celebratory heritage. Earthy terracottas, glowing acacia ambers, and deep emerald vegetation accents rest on warm buttermilk linens.
- **Audience & Context**: Couples planning weddings, corporate planners booking galas, families curating traditional celebrations (e.g., Koito/Ruracio, anniversaries), and diaspora clients coordinating luxury events in Western Kenya.
- **Experience Goals**: Seamless discovery, immediate reassurance through social proof and trust markers, and friction-free direct-to-chat conversion for inquiries via WhatsApp and call-to-actions optimized for high-paced mobile usage.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from Rift Valley sunsets, fertile highlands, and ceremonial attire. The design emphasizes natural warmth through ivory and cream canvas backdrops rather than clinical cold whites.

### Role Allocations
- **Primary (`#B45309`)**: Deep Terracotta Amber. Used for primary call-to-actions, hero accents, active states, key interactive highlights, and authoritative typographic accents.
- **Secondary (`#D97706`)**: Warm Marigold Sunset. Used for rating stars, hover states, secondary highlights, event category tags, and luminous background washes.
- **Tertiary (`#059669`)**: Highland Emerald. Evoking Eldoret's lush agricultural landscape; specifically assigned to WhatsApp conversion triggers, success statuses, verified badges, and catering freshness indicators.
- **Accent Rose (`#E11D48`)**: Festive Hibiscus. Reserved for limited-time offers, wedding focal callouts, and celebration alert badges.
- **Neutrals**:
  - `Canvas Ground`: `#FAF8F5` (Rich Linen Ivory)
  - `Surface Elevated`: `#FFFFFF` (Pristine Milk)
  - `Surface Muted`: `#F3EFEA` (Warm Oat)
  - `Border Subdued`: `#E7E0D6` (Woven Raffia)
  - `Text Primary`: `#1E1B18` (Charcoal Espresso)
  - `Text Secondary`: `#5C554E` (Burnished Clay)

## Typography

The typographic strategy couples the editorial dignity of **Playfair Display** with the supreme mobile scanning comfort of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Display & Headlines (Playfair Display)**: Injected with romantic curves and sharp serifs to evoke the grandeur of curated wedding tents, regal table settings, and celebratory feasts. Titles maintain tight line-heights for impact.
- **Body & Functional UI (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: High x-height, friendly counters, and distinct character terminals guarantee effortless readability on entry-level and high-resolution mobile devices across Eldoret's mobile networks.
- **Location Markers & Micro-Badges**: Small labels use uppercase styling with slight letter-spacing (`0.04em` to `0.06em`) to anchor local geographic tags (e.g., *“ELDORET CBD & RIFT VALLEY”*).

## Layout & Spacing

A mobile-first 4-column layout gracefully scales to an 8-column tablet grid and a generous 12-column desktop structure capped at a maximum width of `1280px`.

### Responsive Layout Strategy
- **Mobile (`< 640px`)**: Single-column vertical scroll with an edge gutter of `1rem`. Touch targets are sized at a strict minimum of `48px` to support quick booking while moving.
- **Tablet (`640px - 1024px`)**: Split layouts allowing dual-column display for menus, venue packages, and split quotation steps. Margin expands to `2rem`.
- **Desktop (`> 1024px`)**: Rich visual presentations pairing wide catering photography with sticky quote calculators and vendor breakdown sidebars.

### Spacing Philosophy
Consistent rhythmic units allow dense information (guest capacity, pricing tiers, dish items) to sit harmoniously alongside lavish photography. Negative space is warmed by background cream hues rather than sparse, clinical voids.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses sun-drenched ambient shadows and tonal surface shifts rather than stark grey drop-shadows.

### Layering Hierarchy
1. **Base Surface (`#FAF8F5`)**: Canvas layer holding structural sections and background textures.
2. **Raised Tier (`#FFFFFF`)**: Event cards, menu displays, and quote forms lifted with warm ambient shadows: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(180, 83, 9, 0.07), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(30, 27, 24, 0.04)`.
3. **Floating Overlays & Sticky Bars**: Modals, bottom booking trays, and the persistent WhatsApp trigger utilize higher diffusion with mild amber-tinted halos: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(30, 27, 24, 0.14), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(180, 83, 9, 0.08)`.
4. **Borders**: Hairline strokes using `#E7E0D6` provide crisp structural definition on cards without relying solely on elevation.

## Shapes

The design uses a balanced rounded corner language (`level 2`) to produce an approachable, hospitable aesthetic.

- **Buttons & Chips**: Formed with `0.5rem` (`rounded-md`) corners to maintain clean interaction clarity, while floating status chips and call badges use full circular pills.
- **Event & Media Cards**: Feature `1rem` (`rounded-lg`) borders, framing feast platters and banquet decor with gentle contours.
- **Modals & Bottom Action Drawers**: Feature `1.5rem` (`rounded-xl`) top corner radii, delivering a welcoming sheet gesture on mobile devices.

## Components

### 1. High-Converting Quote Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("Request Custom Quote")**: Solid `#B45309` with white bold text, `48px` minimum height, subtle gradient shimmer on hover, and an icon arrow indicator. Includes microcopy underneath for mobile confidence: *"Instant response within 1 hour"*.
- **Secondary Action ("Download Menu PDF")**: Outlined with a 1.5px `#B45309` border, background `#FFFFFF`, text `#B45309`.

### 2. Persistent WhatsApp Concierge Floating Action Button (FAB)
- **Position**: Fixed at `bottom: 1.25rem`, `right: 1.25rem` (stacked safely above mobile bottom bars).
- **Styling**: `#059669` emerald green pill or circle with an embedded chat bubble icon, gold notification dot indicator, and pulsing ambient halo: `box-shadow: 0 6px 20px rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.35)`.
- **Pre-filled Context**: Triggers instant conversation with custom parameters based on the current view (e.g., *"Hello Tamu Events, I'm inquiring about catering in Eldoret for [Date]"*).

### 3. Local Trust & Schema.org Badges
- **Eldoret Landmark Anchors**: Visual micro-cards highlighting specific service hubs (e.g., *“Serving Kapsoya, Elgon View, Pioneer, Kaptagat & Greater Uasin Gishu”*).
- **Structured Data UI Pill**: Star rating aggregates (`4.9 / 5.0 from 180+ Eldoret Celebrations`) paired with verified Google Business and physical address badges.

### 4. Event & Catering Cards
- **Structure**: High-aspect-ratio photography bordered by warm raffia lines, featuring floating guest capacity badges (`50 - 1,500 Guests`), cuisine tags (e.g., *"Authentic Nyama Choma & International Buffet"*), and an immediate *"Inquire for Date"* button.

### 5. Interactive Quote Calculator
- **Form Controls**: Segmented sliders for guest count, interactive toggles for decor + catering bundles, with instant dynamic estimates presented in Kenyan Shillings (KES).
- **Inputs**: Generous padding (`0.75rem 1rem`), warm focus rings (`2px solid #D97706`), and persistent field hints to ensure zero validation anxiety on mobile keyboards.
